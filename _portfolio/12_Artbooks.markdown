<i>Bloodflowers</i> and <i>Mistflowers</i> are two personal artbook projects exploring the stories of real-life warrior women and mythological deities from across world history and cultures. 

Through illustration and visual storytelling, these books aim to highlight powerful feminine figures, blending historical research with a strong artistic direction. I handled the entire creation process, including illustration, book layout, and graphic design, ensuring a cohesive and immersive reading experience. I also built the crowdfunding campaign page and managed the production process, working directly with the printer to oversee technical choices, printing quality, and final delivery.

Epireia is a strategy board game created in collaboration with the French history YouTuber Questions d’Histoire. 

On this project, I was involved in the full development process, from the earliest brainstorming sessions to the final production stages. I contributed to shaping the game concept through thematic exploration, gameplay and experience research, and extensive playtesting to refine both mechanics and player experience. 

I also led the artistic direction, including graphic research and illustration, ensuring a strong and coherent visual identity. Beyond game design and visuals, I took part in the crowdfunding preparation and campaign management, including communication assets and social media strategy. I also followed the project through pre-production and manufacturing, handling production control to ensure quality and consistency from prototype to final product.
In addition, I worked on the merchandising products released alongside the game, as well as print installations and promotional materials designed for events and campaign communication.
